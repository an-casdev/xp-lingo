★ THE ORIGIN OF THE PARTY
Long ago, the English Kingdom was sealed behind the Wall of Fear.
Millions could see it from afar — its towers of Vocabulary, its roads of Grammar,
its skies of Pronunciation. But none dared enter.

LYRA was its first Guardian. She watched travelers arrive at the gate,
freeze with doubt, and turn back. For centuries she waited for a hero
who would not stop at the wall.

CRUX was once a warrior of a neighboring kingdom who tried to invade.
He failed — because his grammar was wrong. NYEH. After years in the Grammar Dungeon,
he became its keeper. Now he tests those who dare enter.

ZACK never studied English. He learned it word by word,
deal by deal, in the markets of Vocabulary Village.
He knows every word. Every slang. Every shortcut.

NOVA left home at 18 with nothing but a backpack and a phrasebook.
She crossed 12 countries. She came back fluent, fearless, and changed.
Now she guides others to do the same.

And then... you arrived.
